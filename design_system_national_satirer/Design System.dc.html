05 · Voice & Rules
Do
  • SCREAM in Anton caps for every headline.
  • Quote anonymous "sources" who then give their full name.
  • Hard newsprint edges — zero border-radius.
  • Undercut every claim with a footnote of doubt.
  • Wire fake stories to the owner's real sites.
Don't
  • Use more than two accent colors on one panel.
  • Round corners or add soft pastel gradients.
  • Punch down — roast affectionately, keep it silly.
  • Let a claim stand without a disclaimer.
  • Forget prefers-reduced-motion.
